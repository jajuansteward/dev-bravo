# Restaurants in North Alabama
> The Restaurants in North Alabama is about finding three well-known restaurants in the Florence/North Alabama area.

## authorship
- JaJuan Steward | ([https://jajuansteward.github.io/dev-bravo/](https://jajuansteward.github.io/dev-bravo/)) | 11/10/26 | version 2.0

## user story 
- **As a** person new to the area, they want to find good restaurants to eat in the city.
- **I want** users to find the best well-known restaurants in the North Alabama Area
- **so that** way they will not be wondering where the best restaurants are at.

## narrative
- Well known restaurants in North Alabama!!
- There are different restaurants to choose from steakhouses, buffets, and fast food.
- Are you ready to try the restaurants that will make you full?
